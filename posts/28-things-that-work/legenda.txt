Everything that actually works for me sounds wrong out loud.

Doing the task badly on purpose, because a terrible version that exists
beats a perfect one that does not. Stopping mid sentence, so the open loop
drags me back tomorrow without any willpower involved. Setting a timer to
stop rather than to start, because unlimited is what my brain hears as
impossible.

And the most effective one on the list is having somebody in the room
doing absolutely nothing. They do not help. They do not talk. The thing
gets done anyway.

None of this is in a productivity book. All of it beats the advice that is.

What is your one that sounds ridiculous and works anyway?
