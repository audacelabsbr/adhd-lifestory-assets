There is a name for the reaction that feels ten times bigger than what
caused it, and almost nobody uses it.

One piece of criticism. A message that reads colder than usual. It can
take the whole day, and knowing it is disproportionate does not make it
stop.

It rarely announces itself. It shows up as not sending the work, quitting
right before the finish line, or replaying a conversation for three days.

If this is familiar, it is worth talking to someone who works with adult
ADHD. Save it either way.
