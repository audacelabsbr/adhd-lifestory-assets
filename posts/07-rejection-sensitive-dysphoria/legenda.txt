There's a name for the reaction that feels ten times bigger than what caused it, and I wish someone had told me at 19.

One comment on a piece of work. A "k." where there used to be "sounds good!". That can take my whole day, and knowing it's out of proportion doesn't turn the volume down even a little.

For me it rarely looked like crying. It looked like not sending the file, quitting right before the finish line, or replaying a two minute conversation for three days, with commentary.

If this sounds familiar, it's worth talking to someone who works with adult ADHD. Save it either way, future you will thank you, eventually, after overthinking it.
