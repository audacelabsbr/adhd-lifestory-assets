For years I thought I was lazy. Turns out lazy guys don't rebuild their entire filing system at 2am for fun. I was just waiting for a start signal that never came on its own.

I wasn't too sensitive, I was hearing things at a volume nobody else could. I didn't "fail to apply myself", I spent every class holding my attention in place by hand while everyone else's stayed put for free.

Getting the name right didn't hand me a new problem. It put the correct label on the old one, and things got easier once I stopped fighting the wrong thing.
