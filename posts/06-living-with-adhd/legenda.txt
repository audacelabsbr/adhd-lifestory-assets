Most fights about ADHD at home are not about the mess. They are about
what the mess is assumed to mean.

Laundry left halfway is not indifference. Running late is not a comment
on how much your time matters. Words that come out wrong arrived before
the thinking did.

None of that makes it easier to live with. It just makes it possible to
talk about without one person being the villain.
