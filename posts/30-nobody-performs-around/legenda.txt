There are people I can go quiet around and nothing happens. No follow up
question about whether I am alright. The silence just sits there being
ordinary, and that is the whole luxury.

I can lose the thread mid sentence and they wait, or finish it, or move
on. I can cancel at four in the afternoon on a plan made three weeks ago
and next time still exists. I can talk about the thing nobody cares about
for forty minutes and they are not interested in the thing, they are
interested in me being like that.

Six hours with them costs less than forty minutes of being pleasant
somewhere else. That difference is how I know which is which now.

Send this to the person you stopped performing around, so they know that
is what they did.
