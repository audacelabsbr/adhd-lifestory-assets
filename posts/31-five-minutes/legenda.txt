There are two settings in my head: now, and not now. Five minutes, two hours and next Thursday all go in the same box, and nothing in that box has a size.

Everything takes twenty minutes. Cleaning the kitchen, twenty minutes. Rewriting the whole campaign deck, twenty minutes. Four years of taxes, also twenty minutes, somehow.

I'm not late because I left late. I'm late because at some point I decided there was time for one more thing, and that decision was made by someone with no access to a clock.

It has a name, time blindness. Knowing it didn't fix it, but it ended the argument about whether I was doing it on purpose.

Send this to whoever thinks you're always late on purpose.
