Every organizing system I ever bought was designed for someone who remembers that things exist.

Cabinet doors are where my stuff goes to die, so everything lives on open shelves now. The key bowl sits on the floor by the door, because that's where my hand actually lets go. There are chargers in three rooms because duplicates are cheaper than the twenty minutes I spend looking, and yes, I did the math, it's literally my job.

It looks less tidy than it used to. It works about four times better. My apartment doesn't have to look organized, it has to work when I'm tired.
