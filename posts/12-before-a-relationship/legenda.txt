The first months of dating me are honestly great. I remember everything you said, I plan things, I'm fun. Then the new wears off and the real me shows up with the laundry.

It's not that I stopped caring. The shiny part fades for everyone, I just feel the drop harder, and what comes next tends to get read as not caring.

The other side has a cost too, and almost nobody says it out loud: feeling alone sitting next to someone, getting tired of picking things up, feeling guilty for being annoyed at something nobody chose.

Send this to whoever is dating an ADHDer and wants to get it right.
