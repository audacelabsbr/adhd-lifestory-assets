Choosing what to watch by mood never works. Choosing by what kind of day
you had works every time.

Hyperfocus day takes a long documentary with a rabbit hole in it. Burnt
out day needs short episodes and nothing to keep track of. Grey day is
the thing you have already seen four times, precisely because there are
no surprises left in it.

The right screen on the right day is also looking after your head.

Save this for the next time you spend forty minutes scrolling the menu
and watch nothing.
