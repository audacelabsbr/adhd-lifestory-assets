The internet turned ADHD into a personality quirk, and that version of
the story is doing real damage.

Hyperfocus is not a superpower when it makes you miss the thing that
actually mattered. Twenty unfinished projects is not eccentricity. And
feeling everything at full volume costs you people, not just moments.

None of this means ADHD is a tragedy. It means it is a real condition,
and the fun version pushes the people living it further into the corner.

If someone in your life keeps hearing the meme version, send this to them.
