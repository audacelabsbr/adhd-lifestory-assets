Most of what goes wrong when I'm dating someone isn't about effort. It's the gap between what they meant and what actually reached me.

Hints don't land. I'm great at reading a marketing brief and terrible at reading "I'm fine". Subtle is exactly where my attention drops out. And the good day followed by the distant one happens inside my head, it's not a verdict on the person next to me.

Saying the arrangement out loud, like two adults with a shared doc, kills most fights before they start.
