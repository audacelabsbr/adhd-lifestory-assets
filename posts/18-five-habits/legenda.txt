None of these ask you to be more disciplined. They remove the decision
that was blocking the thing.

One default meal, always the same, so hunger never becomes another
problem to solve. Three minutes of movement before deciding whether you
are training. Opening the file counts as starting, and you are allowed
to stop right there.

The sleep one is the one nobody expects: set an alarm to stop, not an
alarm to sleep. The bedtime never worked because the brain was still
being fed at midnight.
