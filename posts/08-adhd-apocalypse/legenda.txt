The world is ending and my first thought is: where's my charger.

Then I organize the desk I've ignored since March, open twelve tabs about how bunkers get fresh air, and finally answer a client email from three weeks ago, because apparently the apocalypse was the deadline I needed.

Urgency never shows up in the order other people expect. It just shows up, usually with snacks.

If the world ended today, what would your version be doing? The more useless, the better.
