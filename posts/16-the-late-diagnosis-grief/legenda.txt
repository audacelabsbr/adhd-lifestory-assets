Nobody warned me that finally having a name for it would come with grief attached.

Not about the name. About the years before it, and the version of my life that might have gone a little differently if someone had connected the dots earlier.

The report cards that said "bright but doesn't apply himself". The job I left in a mess. The same criticism in different voices. They stopped being separate stories and turned into one that finally made sense.

It got lighter with time. These days I mostly use those report cards as material.
