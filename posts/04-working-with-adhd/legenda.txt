Working with someone who has ADHD is not the difficult part. Guessing
what is happening is.

They will solve the thing everyone was avoiding, then lose two days to
an email. They will answer in nine seconds or in nine days. Neither one
is about how much they care.

A clear deadline and one big task cut into four pieces usually fixes more
than any conversation about focus ever will.

Send this to whoever you share a deadline with.
