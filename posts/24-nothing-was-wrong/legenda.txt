You were not lazy. You were waiting for a start signal that never arrived
on its own.

You were not too sensitive. You were feeling it at a volume nobody around
you could hear. You did not fail to apply yourself, you spent the whole
lesson holding your attention in place by hand while everyone else's
stayed put for free.

A diagnosis does not hand you a new problem. It takes the one you already
had and puts the correct name on it, and everything after that gets
easier because you finally stop fighting the wrong thing.
