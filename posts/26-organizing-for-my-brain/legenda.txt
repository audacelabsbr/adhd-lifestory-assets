Every organizing system I ever bought was built for somebody who remembers
that things exist.

Cupboard doors are where my belongings go to die, so everything lives on
open shelves now. The key bowl is on the floor by the door, because that
is where my hand actually lets go, not where a key bowl is supposed to be.
There are chargers in three rooms because duplicates are cheaper than the
twenty minutes I spend looking.

It looks less tidy than it used to. It works about four times better.

The house does not have to look organized. It has to work when I am tired.

What is the one thing you moved that fixed a problem you had for years?
