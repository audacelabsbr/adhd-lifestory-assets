Nobody warns you that ADHD comes with its own love language.

It forgets the date and remembers the coffee order. It talks over you
because it got excited, not because it stopped listening. It goes quiet
for three days and then shows up with the exact thing you mentioned once.

The people who love us are not asking for less. They are asking for a
translation, and most of us were never taught how to give one.

Which of these did you recognize?
