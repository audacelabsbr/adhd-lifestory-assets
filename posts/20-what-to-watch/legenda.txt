Picking what to watch by mood never worked for me. Picking by what kind of day I had works every time.

Hyperfocus day gets a three hour documentary with a rabbit hole in it, and I come out knowing way too much about submarines. Fried day needs short episodes and zero plot to follow. Grey day is the show I've seen four times, exactly because nothing in it can surprise me.

Save this for the next time you spend forty minutes scrolling the menu and then watch nothing. Not that I'd know.
