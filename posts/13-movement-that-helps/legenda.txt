Movement is the cheapest lever available for an ADHD brain, and the only
one that also fixes the sleep.

Running for fifteen minutes takes the edge off the restlessness. Martial
arts demand exactly the things that are hardest, which is why practising
them moves anything at all. Swimming mutes the outside world, which is a
break most of us never get.

None of this replaces anything else. It just makes the rest of it work
better.

Which one would you actually still be doing in three weeks?
