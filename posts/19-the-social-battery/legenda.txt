Going from talkative to completely gone in twenty minutes is not rudeness
and it is not disinterest.

Conversation is not automatic here. While you are talking, the other brain
is following the thread, holding back impulses, managing whatever the
emotion is doing, remembering not to interrupt, and filtering the room.
None of it shows. All of it costs.

Then pulling away gets read as coldness, so the guilt arrives on top of
the exhaustion, which makes the next one worse.

Have you ever felt your social battery cut out all at once?
