I pay for a gym I mostly visit in my imagination, so I'm not here to preach. But moving is the cheapest thing that helps my head, and it fixes my sleep as a bonus.

Fifteen minutes of running takes the edge off the restless part. Anything with a coach shouting instructions works because I can't drift when someone is about to hit me with a pad. Swimming mutes the world, which is the only quiet I get all day.

It doesn't replace anything else. It just makes everything else work a bit better.

Which one would you actually still be doing in three weeks? Be honest, I wasn't.
