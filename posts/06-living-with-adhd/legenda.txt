Every fight I've had about ADHD under the same roof was never about the mess. It was about what the mess supposedly meant.

The laundry I left halfway wasn't a statement about anybody. Being late wasn't a review of how much I value your time. The thing I said wrong just left my mouth before my brain finished the draft, a feature I'd like to return.

Knowing that doesn't make living with me easier, my old roommate would confirm. But it does mean we can talk about it without one of us playing the villain.
