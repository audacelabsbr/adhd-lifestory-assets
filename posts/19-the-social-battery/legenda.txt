Me at a party: fun, talkative, telling a story about lighthouses. Me twenty minutes later: gone, emotionally already in the car.

It's not rudeness. Conversation isn't automatic for me. While I'm talking I'm also following the thread, holding back three interruptions, managing whatever my face is doing and filtering out the music. None of it shows. All of it costs.

Then I leave early, it gets read as cold, and the guilt shows up on top of the tired, which makes the next party worse.

Has your social battery ever cut out all at once?
