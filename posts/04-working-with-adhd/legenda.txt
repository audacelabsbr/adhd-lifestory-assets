I work in marketing from home, and my coworkers have learned two things about me: I'll fix the campaign everyone gave up on, and I'll take four days to answer "quick question?".

Both are true. Neither one means I care less. The nine second reply happens when the thing is interesting, the nine day reply happens when it's a form.

What actually works with me: a real deadline, never "whenever", and one big task cut into four small ones. That fixes more than any meeting about focus ever did, and I've sat through a few of those with my camera off.
