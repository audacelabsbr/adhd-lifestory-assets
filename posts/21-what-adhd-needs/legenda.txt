Most of what goes wrong in a relationship with ADHD in it is not about
effort. It is about the gap between what was meant and what was received.

Hints do not land. Subtlety is exactly where the attention drops out. And
inconsistency, the good day followed by the distant one, happens on the
inside and is not a verdict on the person next to you.

Saying the arrangement out loud removes most of the conflict before it
has anywhere to start.

If you have ADHD, what do you need most in a relationship?
