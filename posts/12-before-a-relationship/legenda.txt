The first months of a relationship feel effortless, and there is a reason
for it that has nothing to do with the person.

Novelty is stimulation, and stimulation is the one thing an ADHD brain
never has enough of. When that wears off, what shows up next tends to get
read as not caring.

The partner side has a cost too, and it almost never gets named out loud.
Loneliness while sitting in the same room. Exhaustion from picking things
up. Guilt for resenting something nobody chose.

Send this to whoever loves an ADHDer and wants to get it right.
