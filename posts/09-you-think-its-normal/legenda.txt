There is a difference between normal human behavior and a symptom you
normalized because nobody ever told you otherwise.

Everyone loses their keys sometimes. Losing the keys, the phone, the water
and the thread of your own sentence in one afternoon is a different thing.

Everyone zones out in a boring meeting. Zoning out mid conversation with
someone you love, and not being able to come back on purpose, is a
different thing.

Which category did you recognize first?
