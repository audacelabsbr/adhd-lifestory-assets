The big decisions are not the ones that get me. Those come with adrenaline
attached.

It is deciding what to eat, and being no longer hungry by the time I have
chosen. It is a message that takes nine seconds sitting there for four
days. It is forty minutes scrolling the menu and then watching nothing.

The call takes two minutes. Deciding to make the call takes eight days,
and I think about it during all eight.

None of these are big, and that is exactly the problem. They empty the
tank before lunch and there is nothing to show for it.

Which one gets you?
