It looks like it came out of nowhere. It did not. It had been building
since about eleven that morning and I was too busy being fine to notice.

The words go first. Someone asks a normal question, I hear it perfectly,
and the answer is somewhere behind glass. Then standing up gets hard.
Then opening the fridge does.

From outside it reads as mood, so people assume I am upset with them,
which needs an explanation, which needs words, which were the first thing
to go offline.

The shop reopens. It always does. It just does not take requests while it
is shut.

Send this to whoever thinks you went quiet at them.
