The friends who make ADHD lighter are rarely the ones giving advice.

They are the one who asks "didn't you have something today?" with no edge
in their voice. The one who does not take three days of silence personally.
The one who just works next to you and somehow that makes you start.

None of them are trying to fix you, which is exactly why it works.

Send this to yours. They almost certainly have no idea they are doing it.
