Nobody warns you that a diagnosis can come with grief attached.

Not grief for the diagnosis. Grief for the years you spent without an
explanation, and for the version of your life that might have gone
differently if somebody had noticed earlier.

The school reports. The job that ended badly. The criticism that kept
repeating in different voices. They stop being separate incidents and
start forming a pattern that finally makes sense.

Did you go through this when you got yours?
