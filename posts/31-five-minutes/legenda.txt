There are two settings: now, and not now. Five minutes, two hours and next
Thursday all land in the same box, and nothing in that box has a size.

Everything takes twenty minutes. Cleaning the kitchen, twenty minutes.
Rewriting the whole document, twenty minutes. Four years of tax, also
twenty minutes.

I am not late because I left late. I am late because at some point I
decided there was time for one more thing, and that decision was made by
somebody with no access to a clock.

It has a name. Time blindness. Knowing that did not fix it, but it ended
the argument about whether I was doing it on purpose.

Send this to whoever thinks you are always running late deliberately.
